01 What sectors does ATG serve?
ATG works across three practices: Luxury Residential (home theater, distributed audio, lighting, automation), Commercial (LED video walls, conferencing, signage), and Professional (stadiums, venues, studios). We are owner-led and project-led — every engagement is run end-to-end by a partner.
02 Which control platforms do you specify?
We are authorized dealers for Crestron and Control4, and members of CEDIA and AVIXA. We default to whichever platform fits the brief and the client's appetite for ongoing service — never the other way around.
03 Do you work with architects and developers?
Yes — most of our hospitality, transit and residential work is delivered alongside design teams. We can engage from schematic through commissioning, or step in to rescue an under-spec'd existing scope.
04 Where are you based and how far do you travel?
Two offices: Miami, FL and Plano, TX. We deliver across Florida, Texas and the Caribbean — Anguilla, Bahamas and the surrounding islands have all been served from Miami.
05 Is there a typical project budget?
Residential whole-home work tends to start in the low six figures; commercial and professional engagements scale with the count of rooms, walls and venues. We provide indicative ranges based on similar projects we've delivered before quoting in detail.
